# Orbis Arcanorum — offline archive review gate

Internal editorial and monetization-readiness protocol. No publication or live seller approval.

## Every candidate record
- Preserve source title, author/creator if known, canonical source URL, access date, archive chamber, language and provenance confidence.
- Separate verified historical facts from interpretation, speculation and disputed claims.
- Mark copyright/reproduction permission as unknown until evidence is recorded; never treat public web availability as a resale license.
- Distinguish external collector listings from platform-owned inventory. No live sale, auction, payment or promise of availability.
- Require human moderation for religious/cultural context, sensitive materials and vendor claims.

## Offline review checks
- Validate JSON records and duplicate canonical URLs; flag missing citations and provenance.
- Confirm rejected and unverified entries cannot appear as approved storefront merchandise.
- Run existing archive and site tests, recording SHA and failures.
- Confirm the public-facing preview does not expose administrator credentials or private applicant data.

## Release gate
Human approval of sources, rights, supplier agreements, moderation and privacy/security is required before deployment or monetization.
