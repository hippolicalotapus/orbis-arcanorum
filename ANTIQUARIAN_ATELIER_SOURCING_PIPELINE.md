## Launch screening rules
1. Verify exact object URL, object ID, author/date, accession, asset license, download resolution, and commercial rights.
2. Record provenance and flag looted/contested objects or living-community restricted ceremonial items for exclusion or consultation.
3. Do not suggest museum endorsement or use museum trademarks/logos without permission.
4. Clearly mark all replicas as modern reproductions; never imply antiquity or authenticity.
5. Use print-on-demand or made-to-order pilot before stocking inventory.
6. Validate sample quality, production cost, packaging, shipping, returns and Canadian taxes before listing.
7. Never claim limited edition scarcity without enforceable edition tracking.
8. Keep scholarly source links separate from paid commercial placements.
