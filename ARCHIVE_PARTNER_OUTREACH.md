# Archive Contributor and Research Partner Outreach
Updated 2026-11-09. Draft only: no organization contacted and no partnership implied.

## Invitation categories
1. Research partner: source recommendations, corrections, scholarly expertise and community perspectives.
2. Archive contributor: metadata, manuscripts, images or translations, only with explicit permission and rights confirmation.
3. Featured collection partner: curated attribution and links to the partner's website; no copying by default.

## Candidate organizations to research before contact
- Anomalist Archives — anomalous literature and independent collections.
- Hermetic Library — esoteric and historical materials.
- Nichibunken — Japanese folklore and yokai databases.
- Estonian folklorists' Haldjas portal — regional folklore.
- Ethiopian manuscript researchers and repositories — Ethiopian Orthodox and Ge'ez sources.
- Rastafari community archives and researchers — community-led historical documentation.
- University special collections, museum archives, historical societies and smaller regional repositories.
These are leads only, not endorsements or established partners. Confirm correct legal organization, current website, contact channel and permissions before writing.

## Outreach sequence
1. Check for earlier correspondence to avoid duplicate contact.
2. Verify organization's public contact page and the relevance of its collections.
3. Send a short individualized invitation from an authorized Orbis Arcanorum address; avoid bulk unsolicited messaging.
4. Ask for a link or metadata first; request hosted files only after explicit licensing.
5. Log date, recipient, response, permissions, attribution requirements and follow-up deadline.
6. Never imply the organization is a partner until they consent.

## Rights and cultural safeguards
- Preserve creator, collector, community and institutional attribution.
- Separate permission to link, quote, display thumbnails, host images, translate and redistribute.
- Respect sacred/restricted knowledge, privacy and community requests for removal or correction.
- Do not promise payments, traffic, exclusive arrangements or editorial influence.
- No partner may buy a higher evidence rating.

## Proposed initial email
Subject: Invitation to contribute research sources — Orbis Arcanorum

Hello,

I'm reaching out on behalf of Orbis Arcanorum, a developing online archive exploring religious history, folklore, esoteric traditions, historical manuscripts, and lesser-known cultural sources worldwide.

We are building a carefully attributed research directory and would welcome the opportunity to feature links to relevant resources from your collection, with clear credit and direct links back to your organization.

If you are interested, we would also welcome recommendations, corrections, or discussions about a possible featured collection page. We would not reproduce your materials without the appropriate permissions, and we recognize that some cultural or sacred materials should not be republished.

Would you be open to discussing a simple research-source collaboration?

Thank you,
Orbis Arcanorum
