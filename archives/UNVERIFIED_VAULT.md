# The Unverified Vault — Orbis Arcanorum

**Status:** editorial section specification and candidate lead register. No live Tor browsing or hidden-service verification has been performed.

## Purpose
Preserve the history of disputed, speculative, controversial, or difficult-to-prove occult and paranormal claims without confusing them with authenticated primary sources.

## Editorial labels
- **Unverified claim:** No independent confirmation yet.
- **Disputed interpretation:** Sources exist but experts or traditions disagree.
- **Alleged forgery:** Authenticity is challenged; explain by whom and why.
- **Legend and folklore:** Record the tradition without treating the narrative as a historical fact.
- **Unclear provenance:** Ownership or chain of custody cannot be established.
- **Source unavailable:** Claimed original cannot currently be inspected.

## Candidate research leads (not verified items)
1. **Pseudepigraphic magical manuscripts.** Start with Digital Occult Library's manuscript overview, https://digitaloccultlibrary.com/manuscripts/ . Trace each claimed manuscript to the named institutional catalog before adding item-level metadata.
2. **Occult and spiritualist periodicals.** University of Pennsylvania's Online Books Page identifies periodical preservation and Western esoteric collections: https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/archives.html . Check individual holdings and rights.
3. **Hidden-service claims about rare occult documents.** No authenticated document or active onion service identified in the initial public-index search. Do not invent links, provenance, or discoveries.

## Required public-facing fields
Title; claim summary; tradition or historical setting; evidence status; provenance; supporting sources; contrary sources; cultural context; legal/reproduction rights; review date; editorial disclaimer.

## Boundaries
- The Vault is not a marketplace for stolen artifacts, leaked archives, piracy, or prohibited material.
- Do not host files whose rights or lawful provenance are unclear.
- Keep all item records in private draft until a reviewer approves their claims, legal status, and presentation.
- No unsourced accusation about living individuals or groups.
- No unsafe direct links to unverified hidden services.
- Distinguish whether a record is **about** a disputed claim from whether the source document itself is genuine.
