# Orbis Arcanorum — Archive Acquisition Register
Updated: 2026-11-09

## Verification and publishing rules
- This is a source register, not a claim that PDFs or images have been downloaded, licensed, or published.
- Verify each individual object's rights before hosting a copy. Link to the original record when rights are unclear.
- Record title, creator, date, source institution, canonical URL, rights statement, archive classification, provenance, download date, and SHA-256 checksum.
- Classify source type: primary historical document, peer-reviewed scholarship, traditional testimony, or speculative interpretation.
- Do not imply endorsement from authors or institutions.

## Archive XIV research standards
Include both established archaeological findings and alternative historical hypotheses, visibly labeled as such. A researcher's invitation is not permission to reproduce copyrighted books, video, photography or branding.
