## Standard outreach message
Subject: Research and open-access collection collaboration — Orbis Arcanorum

Hello,

We are developing Orbis Arcanorum, a source-attributed digital research archive covering the history of esoteric traditions, religion, folklore, spiritualism, historic manuscripts, and unusual cultural artifacts. Our goal is to help visitors discover primary sources while linking back to the institutions that preserve them.

Could you point us toward (1) machine-readable collection metadata or an API, (2) item-level rights and attribution requirements for thumbnails and downloadable files, and (3) any collections particularly relevant to historical ritual objects, occult literature, folklore, alchemy, mysticism, or related subjects?

We intend to preserve original catalog identifiers, provenance and cultural context, distinguish historical beliefs from established facts, and link prominently to your original records. We will not copy restricted content or assume a blanket license.

Thank you for any guidance or recommended collections.

Orbis Arcanorum Research Team

## Outreach safety
- Search sent/inbox threads before sending; avoid repeat requests.
- Do not claim institutional partnerships, public deployment, 10,000 acquired items, or verified permission.
- Contact official departments only after validating the address.
- Log sent date, contact, response, terms, follow-up date, and permission evidence.
- Seek community guidance for culturally restricted or living ceremonial traditions.
