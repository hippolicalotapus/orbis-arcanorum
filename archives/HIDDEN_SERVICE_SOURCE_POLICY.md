# Hidden-service and darknet research policy

Status: research procedure only. No hidden-service collection or access has been verified.

## Scope
Orbis Arcanorum may consider lawful, voluntarily published bibliographies, public-domain references, historical discussions, and leads discovered through hidden services. A hidden-service address is not proof of provenance, authenticity, legality, or scarcity.

## Safety and legality gates
- Do not purchase, trade, request, host, or redistribute stolen cultural property, leaked private information, pirated books, or unlawfully obtained archival records.
- Do not access illicit marketplaces, bypass authentication, contact anonymous sellers, or transmit payments as part of research.
- Do not execute downloads, scripts, macros, browser extensions, or untrusted attachments.
- Do not put unreviewed hidden-service links into public storefronts or vendor profiles.
- Any claim of a rare manuscript must be independently matched to an institution's canonical catalog, a reputable bibliography, or a documented chain of custody.
- Respect living religious communities, sacred restrictions, and Indigenous cultural protocols.

## Intake fields
For every candidate: discovery date, lead description, claimed creator/date, claimed provenance, public corroborating URL, corroborating catalog ID, rights holder, rights status, risk flags, researcher notes, and review decision.

## Publication gates
1. Identify the underlying item and independent corroboration.
2. Check lawful acquisition and reproduction permissions.
3. Document cultural and religious context, uncertainty, and translations.
4. Editorial review and explicit approval before changing any record to published.

Until these gates are met, record only a non-sensitive description as a private research lead, with evidence_class=unreviewed, rights_status=unverified, publication_status=draft. Never upload suspect files.

## Operational limitation
No Tor or hidden-service access is claimed by this policy. Any actual discovery must be documented separately with verifiable evidence.
