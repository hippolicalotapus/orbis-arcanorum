## Positioning
Independent global archive and emerging third-party marketplace for esoteric traditions, cultural heritage, rare books, archaeology, paranormal research and collectors. Motto: *Omnia Quaerenda. Nihil Obliviscendum.* No editorial classification is for sale.

## First sales funnel
1. Qualify independent rare-book sellers, occult publishers, heritage publishers, museums with commercial programs, ethically operated metaphysical retailers and research event organizers.
2. Record the business name, public website, verified contact channel, topical fit, proposed package and contact date in a private CRM. Do not commit personal emails to the public repository.
3. Check existing correspondence before any message. Send one individualized inquiry through a legitimate public business channel. No mass scraping or duplicate outreach.
4. Offer a no-obligation discovery discussion and a preview of proposed placements. Be candid that the site is prelaunch.
5. Only after site verification and payment compliance: agree in writing on label, creative assets/rights, display dates, fees, refunds, and cancellation.
6. Track inquiries, replies, agreements, active placements and revenue separately. Never report prospects as sales.

## Editorial and community rules
- Label advertisements and sponsored placements visibly.
- Never sell evidence classifications, favorable coverage, inclusion in research records, or implied endorsements.
- Do not reproduce logos, photos, manuscripts or product art without permission.
- Screen advertisers for misleading health claims, scams, illegal goods, discriminatory content and unsupported supernatural guarantees.
- Avoid promises of traffic, clicks, impressions or sales before analytics exist.
- Keep vendor and advertising contracts distinct.

## Launch gate
