# Multilingual Akashic Research Collection — Intake Standard
Status: editorial standard, not a claim of global completeness.

## Scope
Orbis Arcanorum welcomes documented cultural and spiritual traditions from every region and language, including traditions whose practitioners describe knowledge as sacred, secret, oral, disputed or supernatural. Inclusion does not equal endorsement or proof.

## Every source should record
- Stable record ID and original title in original script (when established)
- Transliterated title and English display title
- Original language(s), writing system and region of origin
- Author, creator or attributed tradition; estimated date or range
- Holding institution or source custodian and stable citation URL
- Object type: manuscript, oral account, academic research, image, artifact, interview, etc.
- Rights/license, reproduction restrictions and provenance confidence
- Evidence classification for historical facts, separately from metaphysical claims
- English summary, translation source, translator and review status
- Variant readings, contested interpretations and community-sensitive context
- Last source check and review status

## Translation integrity
1. Keep original wording alongside English wherever licensing allows.
2. Mark machine translation as unreviewed; do not imply it is authoritative.
3. Do not invent missing passages, ritual details or translations.
4. Prioritize qualified native-language and subject-matter review for sacred or culturally sensitive material.
5. Preserve oral traditions with informed permission, attribution and appropriate access restrictions.
6. Do not automatically publish restricted, private or community-protected teachings.
7. Cite original sources and distinguish translations from commentary.

## Quality gates
- Research lead: relevant source discovered; not fully reviewed.
- Source checked: institutional/source page accessed and basic facts checked.
- Reviewed: item-level citation and provenance assessed.
- Translation reviewed: original and translation compared by a qualified reviewer.
- Published: rights, context, classification and disclosures checked.

## Outreach
Seek voluntary contributions and institutional partnerships through public contact channels; verify previous outreach before sending. Never imply access to private collections or endorsement without explicit permission.
