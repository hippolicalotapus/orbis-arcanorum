# Akashic Archives — Editorial and evidence policy

## Classification

- **Documented:** A claim about an identifiable text, artifact, person or event is supported by traceable historical records. This does not validate supernatural interpretations.
- **Disputed:** Relevant sources or interpretations conflict; present the competing explanations fairly.
- **Unverified:** An extraordinary or paranormal claim is reported but lacks adequate independent corroboration.
- **Folklore & Legend:** A traditional narrative or belief, presented with cultural and historical context, not as established fact.

## Required entry fields
Title; summary; classification; topic; location or culture when appropriate; original claimant/source; date of report and date of event where known; evidence offered; alternative explanations; source links; review date; copyright/reuse status; privacy and cultural-sensitivity notes.

## Unverified Mysteries section
Include alleged haunted objects, eyewitness paranormal accounts, lost-civilization claims, anomalous manuscripts, cryptids and occult legends. Use clear unverified labels and avoid sensationalized certainty. Never fabricate testimony, provenance, citations, or evidence.

## Submissions and moderation
No public submission endpoint is active yet. Future submissions require informed consent, reporting tools, anti-harassment controls, privacy protections, and editorial review. Do not publish private addresses, personal identifiers, medical claims, dangerous rituals, or instructions for illegal acts. Respect Indigenous and other living cultural traditions; avoid presenting restricted cultural material without permission.

## Corrections
Maintain revisions and source history. Provide a correction/request-removal route when launched.
