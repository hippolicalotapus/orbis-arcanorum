# Archive Contributor Partnership Program
Prepared: 2026-11-09
Status: outreach planning; no messages sent and no partnerships confirmed.

## Invitation
Orbis Arcanorum is developing a global, multilingual, carefully sourced directory of occult, religious, folklore, paranormal and historical research across fourteen chambers. We invite collection owners, scholars, community organizations, archives and independent sites to contribute sources and contextual expertise.

## Three participation paths
1. Citation and link partnership: submit publicly available source pages with descriptive metadata and proper attribution. No republication permission implied.
2. Editorial contribution: propose annotated records, translations, corrections or bibliographies, subject to provenance, rights and community-sensitivity review.
3. Licensed hosting: optionally authorize specific documents, images or media under written, revocable, clearly scoped permissions.

No contributor is required to purchase advertising, become a vendor or transfer ownership. No promise of traffic, revenue or editorial endorsement. Sensitive indigenous and initiatory materials require community authorization.

## Initial outreach research targets (verify contact details and terms before sending)
- Nichibunken / International Research Center for Japanese Studies: Japanese yokai collections, folklore metadata.
- Estonian folklore researchers / Haldjas: Baltic folklore and local-language indexing.
- Anomaly Archives and Anomalist Archives: independent paranormal research bibliographies.
- Hermetic Library and Twilit Grotto: historical esoteric-text catalogs and editions.
- Internet Sacred Text Archive: bibliographic metadata and links to public-domain works; independently verify each work's rights.
- Ethiopian manuscript researchers and libraries: Ge'ez manuscripts, Ethiopian Orthodox, Beta Israel, Oromo traditions; seek institutional and community guidance.
- Rastafari community organizations and scholars: accurate, consent-based representation of diverse branches.
- Academic religion departments and small regional museums: discontinued, minority and reconstructed traditions.

## Pre-send checks
- Search Gmail and existing project outreach logs for prior contact; avoid duplicates.
- Identify a published public contact method; do not guess addresses.
- Tailor subject, requested contribution, and source-rights conditions to each organization.
- Keep first message concise and noncommercial; do not imply affiliation.
- Log sent date, recipient, response and any rights granted. No automatic follow-up without appropriate cadence.

## Sample invitation
Subject: Invitation to contribute research sources to Orbis Arcanorum

Hello,

I'm reaching out on behalf of Orbis Arcanorum, a developing international research archive covering historical religions, folklore, esoteric traditions, manuscripts and unexplained phenomena.

We're inviting knowledgeable organizations and independent researchers to help us represent their fields accurately. Participation can be as simple as recommending reliable sources we may cite and link to, or suggesting corrections and context for existing entries. We would only reproduce materials with appropriate permission, and contributors would retain ownership and receive attribution where agreed.

Would your organization be interested in discussing a source-sharing or editorial contribution arrangement? If there is a better contact for this request, I'd appreciate being directed to them.

Thank you for considering it.

Orbis Arcanorum
